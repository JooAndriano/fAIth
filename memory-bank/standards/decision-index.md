# Decision Index

Record of all architectural and technical decisions made during the fAIth project.

## Format

Each decision entry includes:

- **Decision ID**: `{DDDD}` (4-digit sequence)
- **Title**: Brief title of the decision
- **Context**: Why this decision was needed
- **Decision**: What was decided
- **Rationale**: Why this decision was made
- **Consequences**: Impact and tradeoffs
- **Status**: [Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded]
- **Date**: ISO 8601 timestamp
- **Related Bolt**: Link to bolt that implemented this decision
